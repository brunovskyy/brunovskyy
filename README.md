# Bruno Pineda

Exploring the intersection of engineering, technology, knowledge and business operations.

My work combines systems engineering with experience in quality, safety, compliance and organizational processes. I am interested in understanding how physical, digital and organizational systems work, how they interact, and how they can be designed to become more adaptable, reliable and maintainable.

Through different projects and specialized initiatives, I explore the development of reusable technologies, physical systems, digital products and practical solutions to operational challenges.

## Key Initiative Examples

### [Mass Builder](https://github.com/massbuilder)

Physical engineering through design, prototyping and experimentation. Exploring mechanical engineering, electronics, robotics and digital fabrication to develop practical knowledge through building physical systems.

### [Netspelunker](https://github.com/netspelunker)

Infrastructure and platform engineering focused on reproducibility, automation, self-hosting and recoverability. Exploring how technical environments can be designed and operated as reliable, maintainable and well-defined systems.

### [Obsiman](https://github.com/obsiman)

An ecosystem of Obsidian plugins, themes and extensions focused on transforming Markdown-based knowledge into structured, interconnected and configurable workspaces while preserving data ownership and portability.

### [PEENO](https://github.com/peeno-dev)

Modular software foundations for building configurable applications, connected data environments and reusable digital workspaces. Focused on reducing repetitive development through composition, interoperability and explicit system contracts.

### [Polysophic](https://github.com/polysophic)

Repository-driven documentation and knowledge publishing. Connecting independently maintained content sources into accessible, configurable and cohesive documentation experiences while preserving content ownership and version control.

### [Primarian Studio](https://github.com/primarian)

Digital product design and web engineering. Bringing visual design, information architecture, content systems and software engineering together to create coherent, accessible and maintainable digital experiences.

### [Ticsum](https://github.com/ticsum)

Software products and tailored applications addressing organizational and operational needs. Connecting business processes, information and technology through modular and adaptable software systems.

## My Engineering Philosophy

- Understand the problem before selecting the technology.
- Make system responsibilities, interfaces and dependencies explicit.
- Develop reusable foundations without introducing unnecessary complexity.
- Preserve knowledge through documentation and reproducible processes.
- Treat reliability, security and maintainability as design requirements.
- Learn through experimentation, implementation and continuous improvement.

Most implementation repositories remain private. Public repositories and organization profiles communicate selected engineering concepts, project motivations, architectural principles and development direction without exposing proprietary implementations or sensitive operational information.
